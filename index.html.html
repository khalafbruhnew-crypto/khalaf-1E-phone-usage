About 1E Phone Usage

This project is about the amount of time students in class 1E spend using their phones in a day.

The data was collected from 19 students in class 1E. Each student gave an estimate of how many hours they spend on their phone every day.

The results show that many students spend around 5 to 7 hours on their phones each day. Some students spend less time, while others spend more than 7 hours.

The student with the lowest recorded phone usage is Khalaf Hakimi, with about 2 hours per day.

This project helps us understand how much time students spend on their phones and encourages us to think about using our phone time wisely.
